By using Moon.Time you are believing in our mission to make sure that your sensitive and important data is protected by staying with and belonging to you. We are committed to putting the control of your information back in your hands, literally. We NEVER collect or track ANY of the data added while using or setting up Moon.Time; that means we can NEVER give away or sell your data to anyone because we don't have it.

All of the data added to Moon.Time stays on your device protected and secure.

Information We Collect

We DO NOT collect any of your information.

Creating A Profile

While creating a profile on the first login, the app creates a local profile and database. Your profile name is stored in plaintext on this device so it can be displayed and selected; your tracked data is encrypted in your local database, and your secret key is never stored. We cannot and do not identify you during this process.
